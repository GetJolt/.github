# Jolt
> Chat should connect people, not trap them inside a digital shopping mall.

Discord used to be the place where gamers, developers, and communities could hang out without corporate nonsense clogging the screen. Then the investors demanded infinite growth, and the slow slide into total enshittification began.

We watched a once lightweight chat client turn into a bloated, memory-hogging monster crammed with popups begging you to buy Nitro, microtransaction cosmetics, profile banners, and sponsored ad quests. They traded clean, reliable voice and text chat for invasive tracking, ad networks, and creepy identity verification schemes. Every update feels like another attempt to milk users for cash while making the core app noticeably worse to use.

We got fed up, so we built **Jolt**.

---
### What is Jolt?
Jolt is an open-source, federated chat platform built for people who just want to talk to their friends without being treated like data products.

Because Jolt is federated, no single company owns the network. You can host your own server, join a community run by someone you trust, and talk seamlessly with anyone across the entire network. If a server owner goes rogue or changes the rules, you can pack up and move without losing your identity or your connections.

---
### Zero Corporate Nonsense
There are no products to buy here. No subscriptions, no digital sticker shops, no paywalled emoji slots, and no ads disguised as mini-games.

We do not track your activity, harvest your data, or profile your behavior to sell to advertisers. The code is lightweight, transparent, and completely free, both as in free speech and free beer. Everything we build belongs to the community, and it will stay that way forever.
